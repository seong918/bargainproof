How it works

A deal is only as good as the price it's compared to. Here's exactly what we check, and what we don't.

Where deals come from

We read the public feeds of the biggest US deal communities and sites — currently Slickdeals, DealCatcher, TechBargains, Hip2Save, DealNews, and CheapShark for PC games. Roughly a thousand new posts a day. We don't scrape anyone's pages; feeds only.

The check

Every deal goes through one of two paths, and the badge tells you which.

BadgeWhat happened
Beats Amazon A deal at Walmart, Target, Best Buy, etc. We looked up the same item on Amazon right then and the deal was cheaper by at least $5 and 5%. We show you Amazon's price and the timestamp.
Price confirmed An Amazon deal. We fetched the product page ourselves and reproduced the posted price — including clip coupons (list price − coupon = posted price). If it didn't match, it went to the filtered-out list.
Community pick We couldn't reproduce the price automatically — usually a promo code, Subscribe & Save, or a Prime-only price. We pass these through only when the community score is strong, and we never call them verified.

What we don't do

  • No price history. We check the price now, not whether it's an all-time low. If you want history, Keepa and CamelCamelCamel do that well.
  • No "lowest across all stores" claims. We compare against Amazon only. If Costco has it cheaper, we won't know.
  • No MSRP math. "70% off list price" tells you nothing when the list price is fiction. We compare against a real, current price.

Why we publish what we rejected

It's the only proof that money isn't deciding what you see. Every deal we looked at and didn't post is on the filtered-out page with its reason — "Amazon already cheaper", "posted price didn't match", "duplicate", "expired". If a paid placement ever slipped through, you'd catch it there.

Freshness

Feeds are polled about every five minutes. Amazon prices are fetched when a deal first appears; the timestamp on each deal is when we looked, not when the community posted. Deals drop off the front page after 7 days but the pages stay up for reference.

Money

We don't earn from any link today. If we add affiliate links later, every affected deal will say so, and the filtered-out list will keep working exactly as it does now.
